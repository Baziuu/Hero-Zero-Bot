## What is VereonX?

**VereonX** is an automation bot for **Hero Zero**. It plays your Hero Zero account for you – training, fighting duels and league battles, running the hideout, doing quests and more – 24/7, even while you're away.

It makes smart choices, not random clicks: it only keeps real upgrades, sells what you don't need, and respects your daily limits. Run the Hero Zero bot on multiple accounts at once and follow everything from one simple dashboard.

> This repository is the public home of VereonX: documentation, FAQ, bug reports and feature requests. The bot itself is closed-source and available through a license.

Works on **every Hero Zero server** – it doesn't matter which world you play on.

## FAQ

**Is the Hero Zero bot safe to use?**
We build in anti-detection tools and keep updating them as Hero Zero changes. But no bot can promise 100% safety – there is always some risk when you use automation on your account. Use it at your own risk.

**Does it work on every server?**
Yes, VereonX works on every Hero Zero server.

**Do I need to keep my PC on?**
Yes. VereonX runs on your own PC, so it only works while your PC is on and the software is running.
